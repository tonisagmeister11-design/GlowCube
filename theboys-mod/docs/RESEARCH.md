# The Boys – Character & Lore Research (state at end of Season 5)

**How confident each claim is**
- **[C] Confirmed**: backed by at least one recap, wiki or press source found during research.
- **[M] From memory**: from my own knowledge of the show, not re-checked against a source this session. Treat as likely but check against the episode.
- **[G] Guess / design interpretation**: not stated in the show.

### Powers
| Power | Notes | Visual (for recreation) |
|---|---|---|
| Superhuman strength, speed, stamina, reflexes, hearing [C] | WWII-era super-soldier and a parody of Captain America. | Heavy, brawler-style melee. No glow. |
| Near-invulnerability and accelerated healing [C] | Shrugs off small-arms fire. | Bullets spark off him. |
| Biological immortality [C] | Comes from **V-One**, Vought's first successful serum (see Lore). In S5 it is also why the Supe Virus does not kill him permanently: at the end of S5E2 he stirs inside his body bag [C]. | n/a |
| **Radiation blast ("the nuke")** [C] | Created by decades of Soviet experiments that turned him into a "walking nuclear reactor". Fired from his **chest**, either as a sustained forward beam lasting several seconds or as one explosive burst. It vaporises humans, chars supes, throws Kimiko through a concrete wall and levels multi-storey buildings. **It burns Compound V out of a supe's blood**, which kills them or leaves them powerless. | Before firing, his chest glows **orange/amber** through the suit and the glow builds [M]. The release is a wide, roaring **orange-white energy beam or wave** with a heat-haze shockwave, and debris is flung outward [M]. Buildup comes with a rising hum and his strained grunting [M]. |

### Signature moves and feats
- **Waking in the Russian lab** (S3E4 "Glorious Five Year Plan" [M]): the first chest blast, which wipes out the room.
- **Massacre at Herogasm** (S3E6 "Herogasm" [C]): the beam vaporises the TNT Twins and carves through their mansion, killing dozens. This is the best "nuke" reference shot.
- **Depowering Kimiko** (S3 [C]; S3E7 [M]): the blast was aimed at Frenchie, Kimiko took it, and she lost her regeneration until she was re-dosed with V.
- **Shield bash and shield throw** in melee, plus firearms (S3 [M]).
- **S5E2**: Homelander wakes him from cryo and sends him after Butcher in Erie, PA. Butcher **throws a car at him** with a tentacle, which cancels his charging blast [C]. He is gassed with the Supe Virus and seems to die, but survives because of V-One [C].
- **S5E6**: hands the **last vial of V-One to Homelander** [C].
- **S5E7**: Homelander chokes him out and puts him **back into cryo** [C]. He does not appear in the finale [C].

### Items and costume
- **Shield** [C]: a **heater/kite-shaped (triangular, pointed-bottom) shield**, not a round one. On the front is an **embossed bald eagle with spread wings and a five-pointed star on its breast**. Costume designer Laura Jean Shannon dropped the red-white-blue scheme for **dark green with red accents**, and screen and replica versions show the eagle and star in **weathered gold/bronze metal** on a dark ground. Lore material: a "proprietary high-density Vought alloy". Finish: scuffed and battle-worn.
- **Suit** [C]: designed by Laura Jean Shannon with Greg Hopwood to look like a "bygone era of masculinity": **moss/olive green and maroon armour plates** and combat gear draped over a bulletproof vest, with a **spread eagle across the chest** (shrunk so the wings dominate). Leather straps and pouches [M]. WWII-style steel helmet [M].
- Firearms (pistol, sub-machine gun) used in S3 [M].

### Weaknesses and limits
- The blast **is not fully under his control**. It builds with his emotional state and panic/PTSD, and is set off by agitation [C].
- It **drains him**: the beam lasts a few seconds and then he is spent. He has to recharge and is often groggy afterwards [C/M].
- **Cryogenic freezing / sedation gas** is the only reliable way anyone has neutralised him (S3 end; again in S5E7) [C].
- Close-range blunt force can interrupt the charge, as in S5E2 [C].

### Visual palette (texture artists)
Olive/moss green `#4B5320`, maroon `#6B1E23`, gunmetal/dark steel `#3A3D40`, aged gold/bronze eagle `#B08D57`, brown leather `#5C4033`, and a blast glow of orange `#FF8C1A` with a white-hot core `#FFF2CC`.

## 2. A-Train (Reggie Franklin, played by Jessie T. Usher)

### Powers
| Power | Notes | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Super speed [C] | "Fastest man alive". He moves faster than the eye can follow. | A motion-blur streak plus a wind/pressure gust. Things around him flutter, and paper and debris swirl in his wake [M]. No coloured energy trail in the show [M]. |
| Enhanced perception / speed-thinking [C] | His brain keeps up with his body, so he can dodge projectiles. | Slow-motion "bullet time" shots from his point of view [M]. |
| Enhanced strength and durability at speed [C] | Impacts at speed are lethal to humans. | Bodies **burst into a cloud of blood**. |
| Hyper-metabolism [C] | He must eat a lot to keep his powers going. | n/a |

### Signature moves and feats
- **Running through Robin** (S1E1 "The Name of the Game" [C]): he runs straight through Hughie's girlfriend, who explodes into a spray of blood and bone. Only her hands are left in Hughie's grip. This is the series' defining image.
- **V-fuelled races and fights**: he injects V before races in S1 [C].
- **Killing Popclaw** by forcing a V overdose on her (S1 [C]; S1E5 [M]).
- **Killing Blue Hawk** (S3, "Herogasm" arc [C]): he grabs Blue Hawk's legs and **drags his face along the asphalt** at super speed.
- **Saving Hughie** from Homelander's heat vision in the S4 finale [C].
- **Death** (S5E1 [C]): he saves Hughie at Vought's "Freedom Camp" and flees. When a woman steps into the road he **swerves to avoid running through her**, the reverse of the Robin scene. That lets Homelander catch him, and Homelander snaps his neck while A-Train smiles and mocks him.

### Items and costume
- **Compound V** syringes and vials: he abuses them to stay the fastest [C].
- **Suit** [C/M]: a skin-tight **blue bodysuit with white/silver panels** and armoured textures, raised shoulder emblems, gloves, boots and a belt. His signature **tinted shades/goggles** are part of the look [C].
- He wears a **heart transplanted from Blue Hawk** after his S3 cardiac arrest [C].

### Weaknesses and limits
- **Heart condition** [C]: abusing V causes heart attacks (S1 and S3). Pushing his top speed risks cardiac arrest. The transplant fixed it in-story.
- Human-level durability when he is not moving at speed. Homelander can simply catch him [C].
- Addicted to V, with a fast metabolism and constant hunger [C].

### Visual palette
Royal/electric blue `#1F4FD1`, white/silver `#D9DEE5`, dark navy accents `#0E1A3A`, smoked-lens goggles `#2B2B2B` with a reflective sheen, and a blood-burst red `#8A0303`.

## 3. Billy Butcher (played by Karl Urban)

### The appendages: name, origin and timeline
- **What they are** [C]: **tentacle/tendril appendages grown from a sentient, V-mutated brain tumour**. The Boys Fandom wiki's term is **"parasitic tendrils"**: the tumour "formed a symbiotic relationship with his body and granted him a collection of extraordinarily powerful parasitic tendrils". Press calls them his **"super cancer"** / cancer tentacles. Homelander describes him as a **"viper's nest"** [C, via a wiki summary].
- **No proper in-show name was found.** Searches turned up no single proper noun. The closest established names are **"tendrils"** (wiki) and **"super cancer"** (press). If the user remembers a specific name, it may be one of these, a fan nickname, or a line I could not verify. **Ask the user what name they mean before hard-coding one.**
- **Origin** [C]: repeated **Temp V (V24)** doses in S3 gave him a terminal brain tumour (12 to 18 months to live, S3 finale). In S4 he secretly takes **real Compound V** hoping to cure it. Instead the tumour becomes sentient, shows up as a hallucination of **Joe Kessler** (Jeffrey Dean Morgan), and makes him black out with bodies piling up around him.
- **When he got them: Season 4, not Season 5** [C]. The tentacles **first appear in the S4 finale (S4E8 "Assassination Run")**. They burst from his **chest**, lift **Victoria Neuman** into the air and **rip her in half**. Before that he couldn't consciously control them. **The user's belief that they first appeared in S5 is incorrect.** They are used far more, and under his control, throughout S5.
- **S5 feats** [C]: in S5E2 he **throws a car at Soldier Boy with a single tentacle**, cancelling Soldier Boy's charging blast. In the finale (S5E8) he grapples Homelander with them. Recaps describe him as able to put out an apparently endless number of tentacles from his chest.
- **How they look** [C/M]: long, wet, fleshy, **dark (black/blue-black) tentacles with spiky, snake-like heads**, described as "spike-covered snake creatures" in the VFX coverage. The S4 finale's were described as bluish. They erupt from his **chest/torso**, tearing through shirt and coat, and pull back inside him. VFX (Mr. X) used soft-tissue simulation for a **viscous, organic** look.
- **End of S5** [C]: **Kimiko's radiation blast in the finale destroys the tumour and his tendrils**. In the same fight he finishes Homelander with his **crowbar**.

### Temp V / V24 powers (Season 3 only)
- One dose = **24 hours** of supe powers. **Superhuman strength and durability, and laser/heat vision** [C].
- First use: after learning that Neuman is the head-popper, he takes V24 and **bisects Gunpowder's head with laser eyes**. He also **crushes Gunpowder's pistol in one hand and sends him flying with one punch** [C]. Episode: S3E3 [M].
- His eye beams look like Homelander's: **red heat beams** from both eyes, with eyes glowing red before firing [M].
- Side effects: nausea and vomiting, raised blood pressure, and **fatal after about 3+ doses** [C].
- **They do not apply in S5.** His S5 powers are the tumour tendrils plus enhanced strength and durability from real V and the tumour [C]. No V24 laser eyes in S5 [M; nothing found saying otherwise].

### Signature moves and feats
- **Crowbar kills** throughout the series [C]. The crowbar delivers the final blow: in S5E8 he **stabs Homelander in the forehead and pries his skull open with it** [C].
- **Tearing Neuman in half** (S4E8) [C].
- **Car-throw tentacle** (S5E2) [C].
- **Supe Virus plot**: in the finale, after his dog **Terror** dies, he plans to release the virus through **Vought Tower's sprinkler system** to wipe out all supes. Hughie goes to stop him [C].

### Items and costume
- **Crowbar** [C]: a plain black/dark steel crowbar, worn and bloodied.
- **Long dark wool overcoat** (black/charcoal), dark shirt, often a gold chain, dark trousers and boots [M].
- His bulldog **Terror** [C].
- Vials of **V24** (S3) [C], and the **Supe Virus** (S4–S5) [C].

### Weaknesses and limits
- **Terminal** from V24, then a tumour that takes control of him (blackouts, Kessler hallucinations) [C].
- Tendrils were uncontrolled until late S4 [C].
- **Radiation (Soldier Boy-style) strips both V and the tumour**, as in S5E8 [C].

### Visual palette
Charcoal coat `#2A2A2C`, black shirt `#151515`, dull steel crowbar `#4A4D52`, tendrils in wet blue-black `#1B1F2E` with a purplish-red inner flesh `#5A1A2A` and a glossy highlight, and red V24 eye-glow `#FF2A1A`.

## 5. Lore

### Compound V
- A **light navy/bright blue liquid** in **small glass vials with metal (aluminium) crimp caps** and a Vought label [C]. Props come in **trays/racks** of vials [C].
- Given by **syringe injection** into the bloodstream [C]. Babies are dosed so they develop powers. Adults usually die from it [C].

### V-One (V1)
- The **first successful version of Compound V**, made by **Frederick Vought in the 1940s** [C]. **Most people who take it die**. Those who survive get powers and **become ageless/immortal**, with passive regeneration [C].
- Known V-One supes: **Soldier Boy, Stormfront, Bombsight, Torpedo, Private Angel** [C]. These link to the **Vought Rising** prequel.
- **Look**: a **cloudy, baby-blue serum**, visibly paler and milkier than standard V [C, per MovieWeb]. The last dose is in a **syringe** that Bombsight was hiding (S5E6) [C].
- People with V-One in their blood are **immune to the Supe Virus** [C].
- **Fit for the "V1" item**: the user's idea of a rare item that grants only Soldier Boy or Homelander powers fits the lore. Soldier Boy is a V-One supe, and Homelander gains V-One in S5. Suggest that drinking or injecting it has a chance to fail (lore: it kills most recipients).

### Temp V / V24
- A temporary Compound V: **one dose = 24 h of powers**. Effects vary by user (Butcher: strength + laser eyes; Hughie: teleportation) [C]. Lethal after about 3 doses, leading to Butcher's tumour [C].
- Vial colour: replicas show **vivid green** liquid labelled "Vought Compound V24" [C for replicas; on-screen colour **unverified**].

### Ways to remove powers in the show
1. **Soldier Boy's radiation** [C]: burns V out of the blood. Non-lethal at a distance, but **temporary**, since a fresh V dose restores powers (Kimiko, S3).
2. **Kimiko's upgraded blast** (S5E7–E8) [C]: Frenchie and Sister Sage repeat the Russian **uranium** experiment on Kimiko. Her blast strips even V-One Homelander, and weakens Ryan and Butcher, **destroying Butcher's tumour**.
3. **Supe Virus** (Gen V → S4 → S5) [C]: latches onto Compound V and **kills** supes. Victims' skin splits apart, exposing skull and organs (Rock Hard, S5E2). Spread by **gas/aerosol canister** (Frenchie in a gas mask, S5E2) and planned for **sprinklers** in the finale. Harmless to non-supes.
4. No pill-style "cure" exists in S5. The only canonical non-lethal power removal is **radiation**.

**Recommendation for a "remove your powers" item**: base it on the **radiation / uranium depowering** used by Soldier Boy and Kimiko. Suggested form: an **"Irradiated Uranium Core"** or **"Depowering Injector"**: a lead-lined canister with a **glowing orange-green core**. Use it to clear the player's power cleanly, with a short Nausea/Weakness debuff. It matches the show (non-lethal, and V can be re-dosed afterwards). Keep the **Supe Virus** (a grey/red-capped vial or gas canister with biohazard markings [G]) as a separate offensive item that heavily damages or kills powered players and mobs and does nothing to normal players.

### Vought labs (for a generated structure)
- [C] Vought runs secret V experiments in facilities disguised as other things: the **Sage Grove Center** (a psychiatric hospital in Pennsylvania where patients are injected with V), Gen V's **"The Woods"** (a hidden detention and experiment complex), Homelander's childhood lab, and the Russian lab where Soldier Boy was held. Vought's corporate look has been described as **"Apple meets Marvel"**.
- [M/G] Suggested build: **white quartz/smooth stone and light-grey concrete walls**, **glass panes** for observation windows, **iron bars and iron doors** for **containment cells**, cool-white sea-lantern lighting, **stainless steel** (iron/smooth-stone counters) lab benches, **refrigerators** (white blocks with iron-door fronts or quartz) holding **Compound V vials** (blue) and rarely **V-One** (pale cloudy blue), a **cryo tube** (glass + packed ice/blue ice with a frosted figure, a nod to Soldier Boy's cryo pod), a medical bed with restraints, and a **Vought logo banner** (the Vought "V": a stylised white/silver V on a navy/royal-blue field). Add red emergency lights and blood decals for horror flavour.

## 6. Game design suggestions

### Soldier Boy
1. **Nuclear Burst** (ultimate). Hold to charge for 2 s while an orange glow pulses on the chest. On release: a 10-block-radius explosion that doesn't damage terrain (configurable) and **strips powers** from powered players hit. Cooldown **120 s**; applies Slowness I and Nausea to the user for 6 s afterwards. **Visual**: orange-white sphere/shockwave ring expanding, flame + end-rod particles, heat-haze, screen shake.
2. **Radiation Beam**. A 3 s channelled beam forward from the chest: 25-block range, continuous damage, sets blocks on fire. Cooldown **60 s**. **Visual**: thick orange cylinder from the chest (rendered as a glowing quad strip, visible in third person) with a white core.
3. **Shield Throw**. Throws the shield like a boomerang. It ricochets between up to 3 enemies and returns. Cooldown **6 s**. **Visual**: a spinning heater-shield entity.
4. **Shield Bash / Block**. While held, reduces frontal damage by 80%. Right-click dash knocks enemies back. Cooldown **8 s**.
5. **Super-Soldier** (passive). Strength II, Resistance I, Regeneration I, no hunger loss from sprinting.
6. **Overload risk** (passive). Taking heavy damage while below 30% HP may trigger an **involuntary** Nuclear Burst (lore: emotional trigger).

### A-Train
1. **Speed Run** (toggle). Speed V–X with step-up, and footsteps leave a dust/cloud trail. Drains a "Heart Strain" meter. **Visual**: motion-blur afterimages (translucent copies of the player model trailing behind) and wind particles.
2. **Blitz Through**. A dash of up to 20 blocks. Mobs and players in the path take heavy damage. Low-HP mobs **burst into red blood particles** (Robin effect). Cooldown **10 s**.
3. **Road Rash**. Grab a target and sprint, dragging them along the ground for damage per tick (Blue Hawk kill). Cooldown **25 s**.
4. **Bullet Time**. 4 s in which incoming arrows/projectiles are auto-dodged. The client gets a desaturated slow-motion overlay. Cooldown **40 s**.
5. **V Boost**. Consuming Compound V gives +50% speed for 30 s but fills Heart Strain fast.
6. **Heart Attack** (weakness). A full Heart Strain meter causes 8 s of Slowness IV + Weakness and heavy damage. Eating restores the meter slowly (hyper-metabolism: faster hunger drain).

### Billy Butcher
1. **Tendril Lash** (name configurable: "Tendrils" / "Super Cancer"). Shoot 1–3 tentacles from the chest to hit enemies up to 12 blocks away. Cooldown **5 s**. **Visual**: segmented, glossy blue-black tentacle entities with spiky heads, animated with a spline wave, emerging from the chest in both first and third person.
2. **Rip in Half**. Grab one target with tendrils, lift it 4 blocks, then deal massive damage with a blood burst (Neuman kill). Cooldown **45 s**.
3. **Car Toss / Heavy Throw**. Tendrils grab a nearby mob or falling-block entity and hurl it. Cooldown **15 s**.
4. **Crowbar** (item). A high-damage melee weapon that does bonus damage to depowered targets (the Homelander finisher).
5. **Temp V Mode** (V24 item). For 24 in-game minutes (or about 5 real minutes): Strength II + red **laser eyes** (thinner and shorter-range than Homelander's). Every use adds a stacking "Tumour" debuff, and 3+ stacks deal max-HP damage (lore).
6. **Blackout** (weakness). At low HP the tendrils may act on their own and attack the nearest entity, including allies.

### Homelander
1. **Heat Vision**. Hold to fire two parallel red beams from the eyes: 40-block range, cuts through soft blocks, ignites. Uses heat energy (overheats after 5 s). **Visual**: two solid glowing red lines with white cores from the eye positions, rendered in world space so others see them in third person, plus red eye glow and an impact spark/ember particle burst.
2. **Flight**. Creative-style flight with high speed. Sprint-flying past a threshold makes a **sonic boom** (white ring particle + explosion sound, knockback around the start point). Boom cooldown **15 s**.
3. **Sonic Clap**. A cone shockwave that knocks back and stuns for 2 s. Cooldown **20 s**.
4. **X-Ray Vision** (toggle). Entities glow through walls within 48 blocks, except those behind **zinc**-tagged blocks (could use a custom zinc ore/block). Cooldown **30 s**, duration 10 s.
5. **Super Hearing** (passive). Shows sound subtitles/indicators for entities within 64 blocks.
6. **V-One Ascension** (unlocked by V1). Permanent Regeneration and immunity to the Supe Virus. The radiation depowering item still works (lore: Kimiko's blast).

### Shared items
- **Compound V** (blue vial, syringe): random/common power or upgrade.
- **V1** (rare, cloudy pale blue): grants Soldier Boy or Homelander powers only, with some chance of death.
- **Temp V24** (green vial): 24-minute temporary power, with a stacking Tumour risk.
- **Irradiated Uranium Core**: removes powers (recommended).
- **Supe Virus** canister: lethal to powered entities only.
